\projecthead{07}{Thrust vector control}{Compact rocket steering mechanism}{Completed}
\textit{Design \& simulation}
\par\medskip A compact mechanism that steers a rocket by tilting its engine, designed around a roughly 20 x 4 inch, 200 gram vehicle.
\par\smallskip {\small\color{muted} Tools: Fusion 360, OpenRocket}
\medskip
\subsection*{Packaging \& control}
Modeled the system in Fusion 360 to fit the actuators, controller, and boards within the rocket. Tilting the engine changes thrust direction to counter disturbances.
\subsection*{Simulation}
OpenRocket studies checked system compatibility, center of gravity, and center of pressure to assess the steering design before flight.
