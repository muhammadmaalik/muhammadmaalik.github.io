    Project · satellite

    PsiSat

    A student satellite about the size of a large mug. It is a metal box meant to orbit a few hundred kilometers up and notice short, bright flashes of high-energy light, called gamma-ray bursts. A crystal inside lights up when a flash hits it, and a sensor reads that light.

    I started the project, gathered the team, and wrote the proposal for NASA’s CubeSat Launch Initiative. The aluminum shell is modeled in Fusion 360, with six thin layers of electronics inside. It has not launched. Jackson Boyle and Christian Tumanda are on the proposal.
